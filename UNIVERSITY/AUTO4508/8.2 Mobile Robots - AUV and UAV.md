# Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAV)
- Flying robots 
	- Autonomous planes
	- Autonomous helicopters
	- Autonomous blimps
	- Autonomous balloons
	- Drones
### Sensors
- GPS - Identifying plane's position to follow waypoints
- Digital Compass - Identify heading
- IMU/Gyro - Inertial Measurement Unit (accelerometers and magnetometers)
- LiDAR - Ground distance or obstacle detection
- Payload sensors - e.g. multispectral cameras
# Flight Controller
- Pre-flight
	- Initialise and test all sensors
		- Calibrate sensors
	- Initialise and test all servos
		- Enable setting of zero positions of servos
		- Enable setting of maximum angles of servos
- In Flight (continuous loop)
	- Generate desired heading
	- Set plane servos according to desired heading
	- Record flight data from sensors
- Post Flight
	- Perform flight data upload
# Multirotor Systems
- Quadcopter, Hexacopter, Octocopter
- Very stable
- Usually electric
- Indoor or outdoor usage
### Indoor Systems
- Equip the room with cameras
- Positional markers on all multirotor systems
- Perfect control possible
### Outdoor Systems
- Sensors needed on the multirotor (GPS, IMU, camera, etc)
- A lot more difficult to manoeuvre
- Large number of potential applications
