# Motor and Encoder 
- Motor speed determined by: supplied voltage
- Motor direction determined by: polarity of supplied voltage
- Cannot generate analog power signal directly from microcontroller
	- Use external amplifier (pulse-width modulation)

# Digital/Analog Converter and Pulse-Width Modulation
- Convert a digital output (number) into an analog voltage (0 - 5V)
- Hardware: Voltage divider ("resistor grave")
- Software: Switching power on/off in intervals, this is pulse-width modulation (PWM)

# Servos
- A servo is a unit combining motor and simple feedback electronics for positioning control
- A servo is set by supplying a PWM signal of a certain ratio
- Ratio determines servo position, not speed
- Three cables: power, ground and PWM signal

# Stepper Motors
- Do not require feedback
- Can be incrementally driven, one step at a time, forward or backward
- Step sequence can be very fast, resulting in a smooth motion

# Motor Control
- Use feedback control to make sure the desired speed is maintained under load
	- If motor goes too slow increase speed and inverse
- How is this done?
	- Read current speed
	- Compare with desired speed
	- Change motor output accordingly

# Velocity Control and Position Control
- Velocity control is making a motor drive at a certain speed and make it maintain that speed
- Position control also incorporates a start/stop phase and makes the motor stop at a certain location
