# Steering Geometry
- The steering is angled so that as the bike leans, the front wheel steers into the lean to minimise the machine's gravitational potential energy
- By misadjusting only the trail, or only the front-wheel gyro, or only the front-assembly mass position, any self-stable bicycle can be made unstable
