# Mass Moments of Inertia About an Axis
- All particles of the body move in parallel planes normal to rotation axis $O-O$ 
$$I = \int r^{2}dm, I = \sum\limits r_{i}^{2}m_{i}$$
- If the density $\rho$ is constant: $$I = \int r^{2}dV$$
## Radius of Gyration
$$k=\sqrt{\frac{I}{m}}, I = k^{2}m$$
## Transfer of Axes
- If M.O.I. is known about an axis passing through the mass centre, it can be determined easily about any parallel axis$$I = \bar{I}+md^{2}$$
$$k^{2}=\bar{k^{2}}+d^{2}$$
## Composite Bodies
- The M.M.O.I. is the sum of the M.O.I. of individual parts about the same axis
# Products of Inertia
$$I_{xy}= I_{yx}=\int xy .dm = I_{x_{0}y_{0}}+md_{x}d_{y}...$$
- Where $I_{{x_0}{y_{0}}}=\bar{I_{xy}}$ 
$$I_{M}=I_{xx}l^{2}+I_{yy}m^{2}+I_{zz}n^{2}-2I_{xy}lm-2I_{xz}ln - 2I_{yz}mn$$
	- The directional cosines of $O-M$ are $l,m,n$ 
# Angular Momentum
$$\mathbf H_{0}= \sum\limits^{N}_{i=1}\mathbf r_{i}\times m_{i}\mathbf v_{i} =  \sum\limits^{N}_{i=1}\mathbf r_{i} m_{i}\times(\omega \times \mathbf r_{i})$$
$$\mathbf H_{0}=\sum\limits^{N}_{i=1}m_{i}[(r_{i}r_{i})\omega - (r_{i}\omega)r_{i}]$$
- Tensor means to denote quantities like stress, strain and the inertia matrix
