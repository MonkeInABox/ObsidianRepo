# Genetic Algorithms
- Apply evolution process to software  
	- A form of unsupervised learning  (“learning” from generation to generation, not within individual)  
	- An optimization technique  
	- A problem solving technique  
	- Maintain a number of individuals  
	- Apply genetic operators to recombine  for next generation
- Fitness Function
	- Evaluate each phenotype's performance with number
- Selection scheme
	- Select individuals for genetic operation depending on their fitness value (e.g. weighted random selection)
	- Maybe preserve best performing individuals unchanged
- Genetic operators
	- Crossover 
	- Mutation
![[Pasted image 20260429112903.png]]
1. Randomly initialize a population of n chromosomes.  
2. While the terminating criteria have not been satisfied:  
	a. For all n chromosomes determine their fitness:  
		i. Construct the phenotype (e.g. simulated robot) corresponding to the encoded genotype (chromosome).  
		ii. Evaluate the phenotype (e.g. measure the simulated robot’s walking abilities), in order to determine its fitness.  
	b. For n/2 times  
		i. Apply selection scheme to gene pool, considering fitness (chrom. A)  
		ii. Apply selection scheme to gene pool, considering fitness (chrom. B)  
		iii. Apply crossover to selected pair A, B to form next generation chrom. a, b  
		iv. With low probability, apply mutation to a, b
