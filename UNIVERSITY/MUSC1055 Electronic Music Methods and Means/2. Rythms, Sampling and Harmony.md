# Rythms and Beat Making
- A bar is our basic container for time
- A clip in Live can be any number of bars and beats
## Four to the Floor
- Kick drum on every beat
- Carries the pulse directly to the listener
- Creates a frame for other instruments and passive tracks
## The Backbeat
- Beats 2 and 4
- The convention is a snare/clap/clave hit to create a driving rythmic feel
## Velocity
- Everything at the same volumn is very robotic
## Syncopation
- Emphasis where the ear doesn't expect it
- Dembow rythm
- 3 + 3 + 2 pattern
## Dotted Rythms
- 1.5x length of note
- Lend towards syncopation
## Polyrythms
- Superimposition of two or rythmic meters on top of each other
- 2:3 is a hemiola
## Polymetre
- Use of two different metres simultaneously
- Same feel, but different groupings of musical phrases or ideas
## Half-time and double time
- Describes where the back beat follows later at half time

# Harmony and Tonal Material
## What is a Key
- 12 notes available
- A key is a subset
- Semitone is a halfstep
	- Enharmonic is Db versus C#
- TTSTTTS = Major
- TSTTSTT = Minor
- Live can help you stay in key -> top left
	- Hit scale
- Triad = 3 notes spaced one space apart
- Wavetable midi effect
- Bass holds or repeats a single note while chord moves around it
- Simple harmony in live
## Melody and Tonal Hierarchy
- Shape and tessalation is as important for melody as are the notes
