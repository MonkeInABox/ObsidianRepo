- Focus, avoid crisis and to make money
- A temporary endeavour undertaken to create a unique product or service
- Project management is the application of knowledge, skill tools and techniques to project activities to meet project requirements - keeping control of the situation
## Commercial Projects
- Intent to make a profit
- Real budget and schedule constraints
- Competitive in market
## Two Options of Methodologies
- PMBOK Project Management Body of Knowledge
	- Project integration, scope, schedule, cost, quality, human resources, communications, risk, procurement
- PRINCE2 - Projects in Controlled Environments is a different one
## Processes
- Initiating: scope, need
- Planning: resources, schedule
- Executing: apply knowledge
- Monitor and Control: check progress, cost, scope, quality
- Closing: finalise contracts, close budgets, record lessons
## Agile
- Sprints
- Time, change, challenges
## Triple Constraints
- Scope, cost, schedule
- Don't change unless contract changes (iron triangle)

- Docs - Contract, Plans, etc

- Product based items (how do we know when an item is finished)
- Diagrams are great communication tools

- What resources, have some measurement
