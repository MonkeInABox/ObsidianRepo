# Why Computer Ethics
- Technology has consequences
	- "Integrity without knowledge is weak and useless, and knowledge without integrity is dangerous and dreadful" - Samuel Johnson
- To behave like responsible professionals
- How to avoid abuse and catastrophes
- Policy vacuums
- IT creates novel ethical issues
# Framework for Thinking Ethically
- Ethics is not
	- Feelings
	- Religion
	- Law
	- Culturally accepted norms
	- Science
- Approaches:
	- Utilitarian
	- Rights
	- Fairness
	- Common good
	- Virtue
- Decision Framework:
	- Recognise issue
		- Problem statement
		- Who is affected
	- Get facts
	- Evaluate alternative courses of actions and consider which actions follow which approaches
		- What laws, guidelines, ethics, values apply
	- Make a decision and test it
	- Act and reflect

# Criteria for a Professional Body
- Specialised knowledge on which others have to place dependence and have to trust those professionals
- It is not a business, union, association or club
- It is a group of people with an expertise and professional skills
