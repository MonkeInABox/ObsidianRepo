# RACI Matrix
- Classifying stakeholders into key groups
- Responsible
- Accountable
- Consulted
- Informed
### Difference Between Accountability and Responsibility
- Responsibility is about the duties and tasks assigned to someone/people while accountability is about being answerable for the results and consequences
- Responsibility is individual or team
- Accountability is a single individual
- Team or individual reports to accountable
