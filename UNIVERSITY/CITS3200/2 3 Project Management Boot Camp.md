# RACI Matrix
- Classifying stakeholders into key groups
- Responsible
- Accountable
- Consulted
- Informed
### Difference Between Accountability and Responsibility
- Responsibility is about the duties and tasks assigned to someone/people while accountability is about being answerable for the results and consequences
- Responsibility is individual or team
- Accountability is a single individual
- Team or individual reports to accountable
# Scope Definition
- How do you know when you're done?
- Prioritisation decisions
### What and Why
- This is your contract with your client
- The basis of estimates of time and cost
- Expectations managed
- Ensure service management is considered in scope

# Project Planning
- Understanding what and why
### What is involved in creating the plan
- Identify tasks and activities that lead you to key delivery milestones
- Build justifiable contingency into your planning, tied to risk
- Identify critical path
- Assign to members
- Culture of ownership and responsibility
- Ensure dependencies are identified and understood
- Communicate
- Establish status reporting/governance structure and cadence
### What happens when it is done poorly
- No one takes ownership
- An unrealistic plan is developed and endorsed
- Wasted resources
- Milestones are missed, team is blindsided
- The blame game starts
# Change Request Management
- A robust and agreed change management process
### Essential and expected
- Scope definition crucial then you can recognise and measure changing scope
- Project teams must be capable and enabled to assess change
- Project manager must be strong and enabled to push back if necessary
### If ignored or done badly
- Budget and schedule are compromised
- Stakeholder satisfaction plummets
- Team morale plummets
# RAID
- Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Dependencies
- Identify up front and continue to identify new ones and manage old
- Ensure risk ownership is correctly assigned
- Make risk heat map, in terms of probability and impact
### Actively Managed?
- Constant communication
- Assumptions need to be continually challenged until
	- Proven true
	- Proven false
	- Conditions have changed
- Risks need to be assessed and re-assessed regularly to ensure correct impact and likelihood
### When not actively managed
- Become existential threats to the project
- Project constantly in fire fighting mode
- Dependency milestones are missed and no one even knows and an issue arises
- Culture of plausible deniability, leading to ineffective RAID management
