# Keating's Rule (with $\approx 90\%$ accuracy)

> [!NOTE] Keating's Marvelous Rule
> **PREFIX  causes a CHANGE in SUFFIX**
 

Thermoelectric - heat to voltage or electric field change
Photoelectric  - optical to voltage or electric field change
Photomagnetic  - optical to change in magnetic field
electromagnetic  - electric field or voltage change in magnetic field
Magnetoelectric  - magnetic field to voltage or electric field polarisation
Thermomagnetic  - heat to magnetic field change
Thermooptic  - heat to change in refractive index
Electroptic  - electric field change in refractive index
piezoresistive  - mechanical pressure to change in resistance
**If you think this is the same as the next you are an IDIOT!**
piezoelectric  - mechanical pressure to change in electric field
piezooptic  - mechanical pressure to change in refractive index
Electrostriction - electric field to mechanical strain
electroelastic  - electric field to stress change
***Does NOT obey the rule:***
photoelastic  - stress caused change in refractive index
thermoelastic - stress caused change in heat
