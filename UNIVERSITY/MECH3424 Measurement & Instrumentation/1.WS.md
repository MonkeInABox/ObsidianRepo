Endpoint method is just drawing a line between the end points
Bias is where it crosses the y-axis
Linearity is the point that is furthest away from the line and finding that distance divided by the FSS (the percentage of FSS that it is away from the line)
- End point method is perfect for something with a deterministic shape
- Least squares has no point for sub linear or super linear, gives a low linearity but does not show true nature. As this one seems random, it will work with this set of data
