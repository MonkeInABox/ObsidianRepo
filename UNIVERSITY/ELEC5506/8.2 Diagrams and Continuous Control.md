# Piping and Instrumentation (P&ID) Diagrams
- P&IDs are used in the maintenance and modification of processes. During the design stage, they aid in the development of system control schemes, such as Hazard and Operability Study (HAZOP).
- The P&ID is a graphic representation of
	• the major piping and instrumentation without going into the fine detail of specifications1
	• Control and shutdown protocol
	• Safety and regulatory requirements
	• Start up and operational information

# Continuous Control
- Types of control
	1. Open-loop: Perfect knowledge of the process, no disturbances $y = G_p \times u$ hence use $u = G^{−1}_{p} y$ to get control . $G^{−1}_p$ may not be realizable or maybe unstable
	2. Closed-loop: This is a control system with feed-back
- Types of controller: On-off control, dead-region and hysteresis (water tank), linear (PID)
- There is an error involved in control, we need an error in control
- Another important question is how fast do we want something to change
