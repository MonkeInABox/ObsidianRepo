> A K-type thermocouple measurement system must provide an output of 0 to 2.5 volts for temperatures at the thermocouple tip of between 500 centigrade and 700 centigrade. The compensation circuit is shown in figure 9.36. A solid-state sensor which produces 9.6 mV for every degree above 0 ◦C (0 V at 0 ◦C) will provide the reference temperature correction. - $\frac{294}{226}=1.3/10k$
