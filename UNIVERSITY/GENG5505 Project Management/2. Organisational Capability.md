# Vision - Strategy - Effort
Vision > Strategic Plan > Portfolio > Program > Project > Stage > Task > Work Package > Level of Effort
- Compare organisational vision with project vision
	- Organisational long term, multiple projects, while it may change everything should go towards it, the big picture
	- Project vision is specifically for that one project
- To be successful a project vision should be:
	- Understood
	- Credible
	- Motivational
	- Demanding and Challenging
- Effective communication is essential to achieve the above, and the project manager should be responsible for successfully achieving it

# Project Selection Models
- Non-numeric:
	- Sacred cow:
		- Project chosen by senior exec
		- Priority status
		- Empire building
		- Potential lack of organisational support
	- Operating necessity
		- To maintain operational functionality
		- Driven by situational events
		- Fast-tracked decision making
		- Limited budget provision
		- Reduced planning time
	- Competitive necessity
		- Maintaining a competitive advantage in the marketplace
		- Ability to match or win competitors
		- Danger of fierce competition
		- Insufficient industry analysis
		- Little competitor analysis
	- Comparative benefit
		- Applies to companies seeking multiple projects with differing beliefs
		- No valid selection criteria used
		- Highly subjective choice
		- Support for high profile projects
	- Product line extension
		- Taking advantage of market conditions and opportunities
		- Use both data analysis and intuition in the decision making process
		- Increased market penetration
		- Possibility of gaining economy of scale, but no guarantee of market success
		- Risk of reducing current share or profit
		![[Pasted image 20260728111932.png]]
- Numeric models:
	- Payback period = time it takes to earn back the money invested
		- $PP = Cost / AnnualCashRevenue$
		- If the revenue is expected to vary from year to year, add the revenues
		- This model doesn't take into account the time-value of money
	- Return of Investment = the overall profit or loss on an investment as a percentage of the total invested
		- $ROI=\frac{NetProfit}{TotalInvestment} \times 100$ 
		- Takes into consideration the entire cash-flow, but not the time value of money
	- Net present value - projected profitability of an investment, based on future cash flows and discounted at a stated interest rate
		- $NPV=CashFlow \times DiscountFactor$
		- $DiscountFactor=\frac{1}{1+i}n$ - (from annuity table)
		![[Pasted image 20260728112408.png]]
	- Oroject Weighted Evaluation Matrix
		- Consideration of qualitative criteria and cash flow/investment is equally desired or important
		- Focuses on finite criterai with agreed weighting scale
		- Some criteria can not be truly reduced to a numeral
