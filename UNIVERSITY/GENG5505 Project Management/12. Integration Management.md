# Developing the Project Proposal
- Confirms the existence of the project itself
- Enables the foundational understanding of the project
- Validates the alignment of the project to the strategic development of the company
# Developing the Project Management Plan
- All plans integrated into a single comprehensive and endorsed project management plan that defines the basis of all project work
	- Scope management plan
	- Time
	- Cost
	- Quality
	- HR
	- Comms
	- Risk
	- Procurement
	- Stakeholder
	- Integration
	- TBL
# Directing and Managing the Work
- Ensuring project objectives are accomplished
- Creating deliverables in line with the planned work
- Providing opportunities for the project team to learn and develop
- Obtaining and allocate the resources
- Open comms channels
- Performance reports
- Engaging with stakeholders
- Risk events and treatment responses
- Lessons learnt documented
# Monitoring and Controlling Performance
- Comparing actual performance against planned
- Assessing performance to identify corrective or preventative actions
- Recommending follow up action
- Identifying and responding to existing, recurrent or new risks
- Accurate records
- Relevant reports and info
- Verifying implementation of changes
# Performing Integrated Change Control 
- Formalised, agreed and practised integrated change control process from inception ensuring only approved changes are incorporated into the revised baseline
- Written and recorded in variation registers or change logs, change requests nominate the required change along with any justification and impact revisions to the time and cost baselines
- Working in conjunction with other stakeholders, the manager will ensure reviewed, evaluated, approved, delayed, rejected or actioned, and these are communicated
