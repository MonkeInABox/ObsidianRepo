# Fundamentals of Ethics
- Moral philosophy: models/frameworks constructed by society that prescribe appropriate values and subsequent behaviours
- Moral correctness: what is the right thing to do
# 3 Core Approaches
- Utilitarian/Consequences-based theory: based on outcomes
- Deontological: actions judged as isolated things
- Virtue-based: constantly seeking to be a good and moral person

**Engineering Cases:**
As a group, reflect on the following real world case studies from both an engineer’s professional and ethical perspective.
*A) Billing*
	a) The budget for a large project is subdivided into task accounts. The report preparation task has $135,000.
	Scenario 1: You have only spent $125,000. What are the options with the $10,000 and what do you do? Back to client, provide additional features, quality assurance, bonus to those involved (teambuilding, reinvested in team), reallocate to another task
	Scenario 2: A situation similar to the previous example, but you have spent $145,000. What are your options and what action would you take? Reallocate, move money around, cutting parts of project, swap out materials if possible, ask for more money, figure out why its gone over budget (was it justified by the client), rectify, negotiate with suppliers or contractors
*B) Customer Relations*
	a) Your company has been asked to recommend one of 5 potential sites for a landfill location. All 5 sites have significant problems. What are the options and what recommendations would you make? 
	b) You are monitoring drilling of pier holes for a deep foundation (200 feet). You notice that the water draining from the muck has an oily sheen, smells strongly of hydrocarbons and has stained the soil black. What would you do?
