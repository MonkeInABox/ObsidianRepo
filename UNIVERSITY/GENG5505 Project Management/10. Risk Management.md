# Risk
- Possibility of loss or injury
- Situation, problem, enhancement or opportunity having a measured impact (positive or negative) on an outcome
- Exposure of an activity to an uncertain outcome
# Risk Management EXAM
- The iterative process of 
1. Identifying
2. Assessing
3. Analysing
4. Managing and Monitoring
5. Evaluating and reviewing

## Potential sources of Risk
- Internal (controllable)
	- Ambiguous scope/charter
	- Comms bottleneck
	- Lack of reporting
	- Managerial incompetence
	- Inaccurate estimates
	- Lack of accountability
	- Limited resources
	- Poor deliverable definition
- External (uncontrollable)
	- Economic cycles
	- Changing tech
	- Environmental factors
	- Social changes
	- Supplier capability
- SWOT analysis
- PESTELG framework
- Key stakeholders should all be involved in identifying and managing risks

# 3. Analysing Risk
- Program evaluation and review technique (PERT) considers critical path
- SWOT and PESTELG - consider issues impacting the situation
- Expert judgement

- Decision trees
- Impact analysis
- Contingency planning

# 4. Managing the Risk
1. Reject - modify plan to eliminate
2. Accept - address as they arise
3. Mitigate - proactive action to minimise impact
4. Share - partnership with 3rd party
5. Transfer - outsourced to 3rd party
6. Enhance - increase probability and/or positive impacts of an opportunity
7. Exploit - ensure opportunity is realised so that the opportunity definitely happens
