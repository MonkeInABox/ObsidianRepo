- GNSS+RTK and 2D SLAM approaches were rejected due to reliance on significant feature recognition requirements (relied too heavily on knowing that this or that feature was there)
	- Also require planners to develop data
	- GNSS = Global Navigation Satellite System
	- RTK = Real Time Kinetic
- SSM (state space model) to make predictions on point-cloud data
# Key Metrics to Evaluate Performance
1. Autonomous driving time (% spent in drive time)
2. Interventions/disengagements (how often human or hardware interventions are required)
3. Ride comfort (assessed by speed transitions and percent change in steering angle over time)

## Related Works
- Flickering AC lighting severely degrades CNN performance in detecting obstacles, with longer exposure times. However, longer exposure times made blurring and inaccuracies.
- GNSS + RTK relies on outside data
- Point-BERT and Point-MAE, ModelNet40, ScanObjectNN and ShapeNetPart
- Transformer-Mamba: Transformer blocks model local features, Mamba layers capture global dependencies
- LiDAR based high definition mapping, like Waymo requires high resources

### PointMamba Model
- Adaptation of Mamba SSM
- High precision on ScanObjectNN set with reduced computational requirements

- With incoming data from two 16 layer LiDAR on the bus, rotationally transformed to align
- Pre-processing pipeline:
	- Farthest point sampling (FPS)
	- Sequence Construction - ordered with Hilbert curve
	- Patch formation - sampled points serve as key points for a KNN search in the original point cloud. Each key point forming a local patch
	- Normalisation and Embedding - points in each patch are normalised relative to key point, with a lightweight PointNet is applied

# PointMamba Results
- Not yet sufficient for fully autonomous driving
- Small perturbations led vehicle to edge of road
- Hard to generalise
- Attempts through lateral data shifting
- Lateral shift does not accurately model the geometric perspective or account for occlusion
- Tendency to under steer gradual corners
