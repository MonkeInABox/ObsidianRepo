# Generalised Newton's Second Law
- General mass system, modelled by considering n mass particles bounded by a closed surface in space
$$m\mathbf r = \sum\limits m_{i}\mathbf r_{i}$$
- Where $\mathbf r$ is the centre of mass $G$
- $\sum F + \sum f = \sum m_{i}\ddot r_{i}$ 
# Work-Energy
$$\sum\limits (U_{1-2})_{i}= \sum \Delta T_{i}$$
- For a nonrigid mechanical system which includes elastic members capable of storing energy, a part of the work done by the external forces goes into changing the internal elastic potential energy $V_{e}$
$$U_{1-2}' = \Delta T+ \Delta V \text{ or } T_{1}+ V_{1}+ U_{1-2}' = T_{2}+ V_{2} | V=V_{g}+ V_{e}$$
### Kinetic Energy Expression
$$\mathbf v_{i}= v' + \dot \rho_{i}$$
- Where $v'$ is the velocity of mass centre $G$ and $\dot \rho_i$ is the velocity of $m_i$ 
$$T=\sum\limits 0.5m_{i}v'^{2}+ \sum 0.5 m_{i}|\dot \rho_{i}|^{2}+\sum m_{i}v'\dot \rho_{i} \rightarrow T=0.5mv' ^{2}+ \sum\limits 0.5m_{i}|\dot \rho _{i}|^{2}$$
# Impulse-Momentum
$$\mathbf G=m v'$$
- The linear momentum of any system of constant mass is the product of the mass and velocity of its centre of mass
$$\sum \mathbf F=\dot G$$
### Angular Momentum 
- About a fixed point $O$, the angular momentum of the mass system about the point $O$, is defined as the vector sum of the moments of the linear momenta about $O$ of all particles
$$\mathbf H_O =\sum(\mathbf r_{i}\times m_{i}\mathbf v_{i})$$
$$\sum \mathbf M_{O}= \dot H_{O}$$
- About mass centre point $G$ 
$$\mathbf H_{G}= \sum \rho_{i}\times m_{i}\dot r_{i}$$
- About an arbitrary point $p$, where $\rho '$ is the distance from $p$ to $G$
$$\mathbf H_{p}=\mathbf H _{G}+\rho ' \times mv'$$
$$L=I\omega=0.5mr^{2}\omega$$
# Conservation of Energy and Momentum
$$\Delta T + \Delta V = 0$$
